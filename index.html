How to use it

  • Tap with the mouse, just like a finger.
  • Swipe the feed with the mouse wheel or a trackpad.
  • Back: Esc or the app’s own close buttons.
  • Sound: Darija audio plays after your first tap.
  • Start over any time with “Start as a new user”, or in the app: Me → Developer → Start over.
